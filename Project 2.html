"Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam"


🥼Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an eminent scientist, the Missile Man of India and the 11th President of India.

🚀Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

🎓Graduated with a degree in Aeronautical Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technologyin 1960.


"🔭Scientific and Defense Career"


Served as Project Director for India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III) at ISRO.

Earned the title Missile Man of India for his work on ballistic missile technology and the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme at DRDO.


"🎖️Presidency and Honors"


Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, widely celebrated as the "People's President".

Received India's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997.

Authored several inspiring books, including his autobiography Wings of Fire.

Source: Britannica

"A Misconception:"

Dr. Kalam earned a traditional academic PhD. His "Dr." title came from around 40 honorary doctorates awarded for his scientific achievements.
